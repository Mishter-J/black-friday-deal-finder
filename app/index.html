DealHarbor

Real Black Friday deals, checked and graded

Every price was read at its store or in a deals source that names the store. Each deal carries a verdict on whether it is really a deal, and the evidence behind it.

    How each verdict is graded

    • New low - a source with price history calls it the lowest price seen, or an earlier tracked price was higher.
    • Strong price - a deep cut against the usual price, with no evidence it is a routine repeat.
    • Routine sale - the same price appeared in an earlier sale, or it only rolls back a recent increase.
    • Inflated list - the percentage is measured against a list price that was raised, so it overstates the saving.

    Prices were checked on 27 Nov 2026 and can change or sell out. The store page at checkout is the final word.

    DealHarbor lists prices read from the stores and sources named on each deal. Store links open the product at that store.
